About Compass

Version 1.0. Threat intelligence for investigators.

1. Detect
Recognises IPs, domains, URLs and hashes, including defanged input.
2. Discover
Subdomains, linked IPs and related domains from certificate logs, passive DNS and archives.
3. Validate
Checks every discovered name against live DNS and flags wildcard records.
4. Enrich
ASN, CDN, open ports, known CVEs, Tor exit and VPN flags for linked IPs.
5. Assess
Threat-intel verdicts from every configured provider, side by side.
6. Report
Per-user history and downloadable reports with sources and timestamps.
Email headers
Received chain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, DKIM signature checks and Message-ID consistency checks.

What the colours mean

Passive mode is the default: Compass queries third-party services and never contacts the investigated host. Active checks connect to the target directly, so the target can see the server's IP address.
